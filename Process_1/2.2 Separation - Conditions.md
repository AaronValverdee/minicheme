## 2. First-Principles Rationale & Sizing Equations

### 2.1 Column Pressure Selection (`DIST-003`)
* **Heuristic Guideline**: Operate distillation columns at the lowest pressure that permits complete condensation using standard cooling water ($30^\circ\text{C}$ supply, $40^\circ\text{C}$ return).
* **T-201 (Debutizer)**:
  - Distillate is primarily isobutane ($35\%$), 2-butenes ($21\%$), and 1-butene ($11\%$) plus azeotropic methanol.
  - At $1.0\text{ atm}$, isobutane boils at $-11.7^\circ\text{C}$ (requiring expensive sub-ambient refrigeration).
  - Raising column pressure to **$6.5\text{ barg}$ ($7.5\text{ bar abs}$)** shifts the overhead bubble point to **$49.0^\circ\text{C}$**.
  - Temperature driving force in condenser: $\Delta T = 49^\circ\text{C} - 30^\circ\text{C} = 19^\circ\text{C} > 10^\circ\text{C}$ minimum approach (`HEX-004`), allowing 100% cooling water condensation and eliminating refrigeration compressors (`UTIL-REF-001`).
  - Bottoms Temperature: At $7.5\text{ bar abs}$, pure MTBE boils at $138^\circ\text{C}$. This is safely below the MTBE thermal decomposition limit ($>160^\circ\text{C}$), ensuring stable bottom reboiler operation without product degradation.
* **T-203 (Methanol Recovery)**:
  - Operated at $1.2\text{ barg}$ ($2.2\text{ bar abs}$). Overhead methanol condenses at $78^\circ\text{C}$ with cooling water, while bottoms water reboils at $115^\circ\text{C}$ using low-pressure steam ($3.5\text{ barg}$, $148^\circ\text{C}$).

### 2.2 Reflux Ratio and Theoretical Stages (`DIST-004`, `DIST-005`)
* **T-201**:
  - The split is very favorable: $\alpha_{\text{C4/MTBE}} \approx 6.0$.
  - Minimum stages from Fenske equation: $N_{\min} \approx \frac{\ln\left(\frac{0.999 \times 0.995}{0.001 \times 0.005}\right)}{\ln(6.0)} \approx 6.8$ ideal binary equivalent; accounting for multicomponent pinches, $N_{\min} \approx 18$.
  - Optimum operating stages: $N = 1.8 \times N_{\min} \approx 36$ stages (`DIST-004`).
  - Operating reflux: $R_{\min} \approx 0.90 \implies R_{\text{op}} = 1.25 \times 0.90 \approx 1.15$.
* **Internals Choice (`DIST-005`)**:
  - Calculated column diameter $D_i = 0.65\text{ m} < 0.9\text{ m}$.
  - Heuristic `DIST-005` specifies structured packing for column diameters $< 0.9\text{ m}$ to prevent weeping, minimize liquid holdup, and lower column pressure drop ($\Delta P_{\text{col}} < 0.2\text{ bar}$).
  - With standard HETP $\approx 0.45\text{ m}$, active bed height is $H_{\text{pack}} = 36 \times 0.45 = 16.2\text{ m}$.
  - Including feed distributors, vapor disengagement, and bottoms sump (`DIST-008`), total height is $20.5\text{ m}$.

### 2.3 Methanol Water Extractor Sizing (T-202) (`EXTR-003`, `EXTR-004`)
* **Extraction Conditions**: $P = 6.0\text{ barg}$, $T = 35^\circ\text{C}$. Operating at $6\text{ barg}$ allows direct liquid feed from the debutizer reflux drum without intermediate depressurization or booster pumps (`PROC-009`).
* **Solvent Ratio & Stages**:
  - Hydrocarbon flow: $\dot{m}_{\text{org}} = 1,020\text{ kg/h}$ ($1.72\text{ m}^3\text{/h}$).
  - Wash water rate: $\dot{m}_{\text{aq}} = 150\text{ kg/h}$ ($0.15\text{ m}^3\text{/h}$).
  - Mass ratio $S/F \approx 0.15$. The extraction factor $\mathcal{E} = K_D \cdot \frac{S}{F} \approx 30 \times 0.15 = 4.5 \gg 1.5$.
  - Kremser equation yields $N_{\text{stages}} = 5.2 \implies \mathbf{6\text{ theoretical stages}}$ (`EXTR-003`).
* **Phase Dispersion (`EXTR-004`)**:
  - Volumetric ratio $\dot{V}_{\text{org}} / \dot{V}_{\text{aq}} = 1.72 / 0.15 \approx 11.5 : 1$.
  - Heuristic `EXTR-004` states: *Disperse the phase with the higher volumetric flow rate*.
  - Therefore, the hydrocarbon phase is **dispersed** as droplets, while water forms the continuous downward-flowing film over 1" stainless steel Pall rings.
  - Column diameter: $D_i = 0.35\text{ m}$ ($14\text{ in}$), total packed height: $6.0\text{ m}$, vessel height: $8.0\text{ m}$.

## 3. Justifying Heuristic Rules

| Heuristic ID | Heuristic Rule Summary | Key Guidelines & Equations Applied | Source Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `DIST-003` | Column operating pressure setting | Set pressure so condenser operates with cooling water ($T_{\text{cond}} \ge 45-50^\circ\text{C}$); ensure bottoms $T < 150^\circ\text{C}$ to avoid thermal degradation. | Turton (2012) Table 11.13 |
| `DIST-004` | Economic reflux ratio and stage count | Set $R = 1.2 \text{ to } 1.3 R_{\min}$; set theoretical stages $N = 1.5 \text{ to } 2.0 N_{\min}$. | Seider (2010) Heuristic 9 |
| `DIST-005` | Tray vs. packing selection | For diameters $D < 0.9\text{ m}$ ($3\text{ ft}$), choose structured packing over trays to reduce pressure drop and ensure uniform liquid distribution. | Walas (1988) / Ulrich p. 578 |
| `DIST-008` | Packed tower active height sizing | Calculate total height as $H = N \cdot \text{HETP} + \text{allowances for distributors and sump}$ ($1.5-2.5\text{ m}$ top/bottom). | Ulrich & Vasudevan (2004) p. 578 |
| `EXTR-003` | Extraction theoretical stages | Commercial extraction cascades typically require 4 to 10 theoretical stages. Specified 6 stages for T-202. | Turton Table 11.15 |
| `EXTR-004` | Extractor phase dispersion | Disperse the phase with the higher volumetric flow rate ($V_{\text{org}} / V_{\text{aq}} \approx 11.5 \implies$ hydrocarbon dispersed). | Seider (2010) Heuristic 14 |
