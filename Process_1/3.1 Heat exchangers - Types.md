## 2. First-Principles Rationale & Selection Criteria

### 2.1 Tube-Side vs. Shell-Side Allocation Rules (`HEX-002`)
The fluids in each unit are strictly allocated according to established chemical engineering heuristics:
1. **Cooling Tower Water**: Allocated to the **tube side** in E-102, E-201, E-204, and E-205. Cooling water is aerated, contains mineral salts, and exhibits biological/scaling fouling tendencies. Placing it inside straight tubes allows on-line chemical cleaning and easy mechanical rodding without opening the shell casing.
2. **High-Pressure Streams**: High-pressure reaction feed ($12-13\text{ barg}$) in E-101 is allocated to the **tube side**. Tube walls inherently resist internal hoop stress with minimal wall thickness, eliminating the need for an expensive heavy-wall shell.
3. **Condensing and Boiling Streams**: Condensing C4/methanol vapors (E-201, E-205) are routed to the **shell side** to minimize vapor flow resistance and keep pressure drop below $0.15\text{ bar}$. Boiling fluids in kettle reboiler E-202 are routed to the enlarged shell space to permit unhindered vapor disengagement without liquid entrainment.

### 2.2 Standard Geometry & Mechanical Design (`HEX-001`)
* **Tube Dimensions**: All shell-and-tube exchangers utilize industry-standard $3/4\text{ inch}$ ($19.05\text{ mm}$) outside diameter (OD) tubes with 16 BWG ($1.65\text{ mm}$) wall thickness.
* **Pitch & Layout**: Tubes are arranged on a $1\text{ inch}$ ($25.4\text{ mm}$) **triangular pitch** ($30^\circ$ orientation), maximizing heat transfer coefficient per unit shell volume for clean organic fluids (`HEX-001`).
* **Tube Length**: Standard $16\text{ ft}$ ($4.88\text{ m}$) tube lengths are specified for primary exchangers (E-101, E-102, E-201) to minimize shell diameter and shell-flange costs.

### 2.3 Nucleate vs. Film Boiling Verification in Reboilers (`HEX-007`)
A critical operating failure in distillation reboilers is transitioning into film boiling (vapor blanketing), which drops the heat transfer coefficient by an order of magnitude:
* **Heuristic Criterion**: Maintain temperature driving force $\Delta T_{\text{reb}} = T_{\text{steam}} - T_{\text{process}} \le 25^\circ\text{C}$ ($45^\circ\text{F}$) to guarantee nucleate boiling.
* **E-202 (Debutizer Reboiler)**:
  - Process boiling temperature at $6.5\text{ barg}$: $T_{\text{boil}} = 138^\circ\text{C}$.
  - Utility: Low-pressure steam at $3.5\text{ barg}$ ($T_{\text{sat}} = 148^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Overall driving force: $\Delta T = 148^\circ\text{C} - 138^\circ\text{C} = \mathbf{10^\circ\text{C}} \le 25^\circ\text{C}$.
  - **Verdict**: Nucleate boiling regime is safely maintained; vapor blanketing risk is zero.
* **E-206 (Methanol Recovery Reboiler)**:
  - Process boiling temperature at $1.2\text{ barg}$: $T_{\text{boil}} = 115^\circ\text{C}$.
  - Using throttled LP steam at $2.0\text{ barg}$ ($T_{\text{sat}} = 133^\circ\text{C}$) provides $\Delta T = 18^\circ\text{C} \le 25^\circ\text{C}$, safely within the nucleate boiling envelope.

## 3. Justifying Heuristic Rules

| Heuristic ID | Heuristic Rule Summary | Key Guidelines & Equations Applied | Source Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `HEX-001` | Shell-and-tube geometry standards | Specify $3/4\text{ in}$ ($19.05\text{ mm}$) OD tubes on $1\text{ in}$ triangular pitch, $16\text{ ft}$ ($4.88\text{ m}$) length for commercial exchangers. | Turton (2012) Table 11.11 |
| `HEX-002` | Tube-side vs. shell-side allocation | Corrosive, fouling, and higher-pressure fluids on tube side; condensing, boiling, and viscous fluids on shell side. | Seider (2010) Heuristic 33 |
| `HEX-003` | Heat transfer area calculation | Preliminary area calculated via $A = Q / (U \cdot \Delta T_{\text{LM}} \cdot F_T)$. | Sinnott & Towler (2013) Chapter 12 |
| `HEX-005` | Overall heat transfer coefficients ($U$) | Used typical values: liquid-liquid organic $U \approx 350-500\text{ W/(m}^2\cdot\text{K)}$; condensing hydrocarbon $U \approx 450-700\text{ W/(m}^2\cdot\text{K)}$; steam reboiler $U \approx 800-1200\text{ W/(m}^2\cdot\text{K)}$. | Ulrich & Vasudevan Table C-1 |
| `HEX-006` | Allowable pressure drops | Design liquid streams for $\Delta P \le 0.35-0.70\text{ bar}$; condensing vapors for $\Delta P \le 0.10-0.15\text{ bar}$. | Ulrich & Vasudevan p. 580 |
| `HEX-007` | Reboiler maximum temperature approach | In reboilers and vaporizers, maintain $\Delta T \le 25^\circ\text{C}$ ($45^\circ\text{F}$) to avoid entering the unstable film boiling regime. | Walas (1988) / Seider Heuristic 36 |
| `HEX-010` | Double-pipe / Hairpin exchangers | Specify hairpin exchangers when required surface area $A < 20\text{ m}^2$ (applied to E-203 and E-204). | Turton (2012) Table 11.11 |
