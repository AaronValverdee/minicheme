## 1. Preliminary Engineering Design Proposal

The synthesis of Methyl *tert*-butyl ether (MTBE) is conducted as a liquid-phase, heterogeneously catalyzed equilibrium-limited addition reaction:

$$\text{CH}_3\text{C(CH}_3\text{)=CH}_2 (l) + \text{CH}_3\text{OH} (l) \xrightleftharpoons[\text{Resin Catalyst}]{\Delta H^\circ_{\text{rxn}} = -37.2\text{ kJ/mol}} (\text{CH}_3)_3\text{C}-\text{O}-\text{CH}_3 (l)$$

* **Thermal Classification**: **Moderately Exothermic** ($\Delta H^\circ_{\text{rxn}} = -37.2\text{ kJ/mol} = -16,000\text{ Btu/lb-mol}$; $\Delta T_{\text{ad}} \approx 42.4^\circ\text{C}$).
* **Equilibrium Window**: Operating at $50^\circ\text{C}$ to $75^\circ\text{C}$ provides an optimal compromise: high equilibrium conversion ($K_{\text{eq}} \approx 25 - 35$ at $55^\circ\text{C}$, permitting $>92-95\%$ conversion per pass) while sustaining sufficient kinetic rate over macroporous ion-exchange resin.
* **Phase Stability**: Operating pressure is established at $10-15\text{ barg}$, ensuring all C4 hydrocarbons and methanol remain strictly in the liquid phase without localized boiling or vapor pocket formation in the catalyst bed.

## 2. First-Principles Rationale & Calculations

### 2.1 Stoichiometry and Molar Flow Rates
From the Basis of Design ($10,000\text{ t/year}$ naphtha, $8,000\text{ h/year}$, $19\text{ wt}\%$ isobutylene):
* **Naphtha Mass Flow**: $\dot{m}_{\text{naphtha}} = 1,250.0\text{ kg/h}$.
* **Isobutylene Feed Rate**: $\dot{m}_{i\text{-C4}} = 1,250 \times 0.19 = 237.5\text{ kg/h}$ ($4.233\text{ kmol/h} = 4,233\text{ mol/h}$).
* **Methanol Feed Ratio**: Specified at $\text{MeOH} : i\text{-C}_4\text{H}_8 = 1.05 : 1.00$ ($5\%$ molar excess).
* **Total Methanol Feed**: $\dot{n}_{\text{MeOH}} = 1.05 \times 4.233 = 4.445\text{ kmol/h}$ ($142.4\text{ kg/h}$).
* **Total Reactor Feed**: $\dot{m}_{\text{feed, tot}} = 1,250.0 + 142.4 = 1,392.4\text{ kg/h}$.

### 2.2 Heat Generation & Adiabatic Temperature Rise ($\Delta T_{\text{ad}}$)
Assuming a design primary single-pass conversion $X_{i\text{-C4}} = 90\%$ ($3.810\text{ kmol/h}$ converted):
$$\dot{Q}_{\text{gen}} = \dot{n}_{i\text{-C4, conv}} \cdot (-\Delta H^\circ_{\text{rxn}}) = (3,810\text{ mol/h}) \cdot (37.2\text{ kJ/mol}) = 141,732\text{ kJ/h} = 39.37\text{ kW}$$

The average liquid heat capacity of the feed mixture (dominated by C4 alkanes/alkenes with $C_p \approx 2.35\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$ and methanol $C_p \approx 2.65\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$) is $C_{p,\text{mix}} \approx 2.40\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$.

The adiabatic temperature rise is:
$$\Delta T_{\text{ad}} = \frac{-\Delta H^\circ_{\text{rxn}} \cdot X_{i\text{-C4}} \cdot n_{i\text{-C4}}}{\dot{m}_{\text{feed, tot}} \cdot C_{p,\text{mix}}} = \frac{141,732\text{ kJ/h}}{1,392.4\text{ kg/h} \times 2.40\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}} \approx 42.4^\circ\text{C}$$

* **Conclusion**: With an inlet temperature of $50^\circ\text{C}$, the unmitigated adiabatic exit temperature would reach $92.4^\circ\text{C}$. This temperature must be moderated below the resin desulfonation threshold ($120^\circ\text{C}$) to prevent catalyst degradation and suppress byproduct dimerization.

### 2.3 Side Reactions & Extinction Analysis
1. **Isobutylene Dimerization to Diisobutylene (DIB)**:
   $$2 \, i\text{-C}_4\text{H}_8 \xrightleftharpoons{} \text{DIB} \quad (\Delta H^\circ \approx -80\text{ kJ/mol})$$
   *Suppression Mechanism*: Polar methanol preferentially adsorbs onto the sulfonic acid ($-\text{SO}_3\text{H}$) sites of the resin. Maintaining $\text{MeOH} : i\text{-C}_4 > 1.00$ creates a methanol monolayer that inhibits the contact between two isobutylene molecules, keeping DIB yield $<0.5\text{ wt}\%$.
2. **Isobutylene Hydration to *tert*-Butyl Alcohol (TBA)**:
   $$i\text{-C}_4\text{H}_8 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightleftharpoons{} \text{TBA}$$
   TBA is an excellent, high-octane gasoline blending component. Any trace TBA formed is gasoline-compatible and leaves harmlessly with the MTBE bottom product (`PROC-007`).
3. **Methanol Dehydration to Dimethyl Ether (DME)**:
   $$2 \, \text{CH}_3\text{OH} \xrightleftharpoons{} \text{CH}_3\text{OCH}_3 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$$
   Kinetically negligible below $90^\circ\text{C}$ over sulfonic resins; selectivity to MTBE exceeds $99.5\%$.

## 3. Justifying Heuristic Rules

| Heuristic ID | Heuristic Rule Summary | Key Guidelines & Equations Applied | Source Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `REACT-004` | Heat removal for exothermic reactions | Moderately high exotherm; evaluate diluent effect and excess reactant. Excess C4 inerts ($81\text{ wt}\%$ of naphtha) act as an internal heat sink, limiting adiabatic rise to $42.4^\circ\text{C}$. | Seider (2010) Heuristic 22 |
| `REACT-005` | Cooling for moderate heats of reaction | For moderate exotherms ($\Delta T_{\text{ad}} < 100^\circ\text{C}$), utilize multi-tubular cooled reactors, intermediate cooling exchangers, or pump-around cooling loops. | Ulrich & Vasudevan (2004) p. 581 |
| `PROC-007` | Recycle / extinction of reversible byproducts | Reversible byproducts formed in trace quantities (e.g. TBA/DIB) need not be isolated if they meet final fuel specifications or can be recycled to extinction. | Douglas (1988) / Turton Table 11.1 |
| `PROC-004` | Excess reactant allocation | Feed a $5\%$ stoichiometric excess of methanol ($\text{MeOH}/i\text{-C}_4 = 1.05$) to drive isobutylene conversion toward completion and selectively poison the acid-catalyzed olefin dimerization pathway. | Seider (2010) Heuristic 2 |
